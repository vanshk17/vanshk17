## 🦾 About Me:

👋 Hi, I'm **Vansh Kukreja**, a **Computer Science Engineering** student focused on **Quantitative Research, Systematic Trading, and Energy Markets**.

📈 I research **WTI (CL) and Brent (BRN/LCO) futures**, with a focus on **calendar spreads, relative-value structures, curve behaviour, and cross-market relationships**.

🧠 I enjoy applying **statistics, time-series analysis, and market intuition** to understand price behaviour and develop systematic trading ideas.

🔬 My current research includes **cointegration, ADF testing, mean reversion, lead-lag analysis, z-score strategies, event studies, and regime analysis**.

⚡ I’m particularly interested in how **information flows across energy markets**, how different parts of the futures curve react, and where **statistical mispricings or relative-value opportunities** may exist.

💻 I primarily use **Python, Pandas, NumPy, Statsmodels, SQL/PostgreSQL, and Jupyter** for research, backtesting, data analysis, and strategy development.

🎯 Working towards a career in **Quantitative Research, Quant Trading, and Energy Markets**, combining **data-driven research with real-world trading intuition**.
